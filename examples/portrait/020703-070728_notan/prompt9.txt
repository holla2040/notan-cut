Make only these two changes and keep everything else exactly as it is:  1. Close the tiny gap at the left corner of the mouth, on the left side of the image, so the pointed tip of the mouth touches and merges into the black cheek line right next to it.  2. Close the tiny gap at the right corner of the mouth, on the right side of the image, so the pointed tip of the mouth touches and merges into the black fold line right next to it.
